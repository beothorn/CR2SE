The internet has changed the way 
communication works in our society.

The natural limitations for communication
that existed before the internet dictated 
what people and organizations were able 
to do. 

The channels to pass information were expensive 
an the more people you wanted to reach, the more 
expensive it got.

That created a dynamic of smaller communities, 
where common interests were the main topic of 
conversations and input out of the circle 
existed, but was limited. 

Access to these "closed communities" were possible,
but carried a cost, so if one wanted to push 
an idea, it had to be something worth the effort. 

All this changed with the advent of the internet.

The hope at the time was that with global
access, people would easily access information,
leading to a new era of knowledge, understanding and
enlightenment.

That partially has happened, but there were 
side effects. 

The same way people have access to information, 
access to those people became cheap. 

So cheap indeed that pushing whatever you 
wanted to as many people as you wanted is 
trivial. 

Motivated by ads, with time, we consolidated
the structure of the web to maximize access
to a person and their data. 

Because access is the capital of the internet.

The problem is that, as everything does,
we are getting to a point of saturation.

Too many interests are now at play,
ads, scraping, politics.

The internet now is a place where the moment
it is clear you are not a robot, you are a target.

It is time to go back, at least partially
to a point where local reach is cheap, but
global reach is expensive.
